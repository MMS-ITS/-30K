VERDICT

Pay the extra principal and keep the original scheduled P&I payment.

Because the loan is new, the rate is 6.8%, and substantial reserves remain, this is generally the strongest long-term option. Recast only if improving monthly cash flow matters more than maximizing lifetime interest savings.

Before sending the extra principal
  1. Confirm there is no prepayment penalty.
  2. Tell the lender to apply the payment directly to principal.
  3. Confirm whether the reported $2,000 monthly lender payment includes escrow, taxes, insurance or other charges.
  4. Keep adequate vacancy, repair, insurance and personal emergency reserves.
  5. Use Form 1098 and actual closing/tax records for the tax return.
Default assumptions: $300,000 purchase price, 30% down, 6.8% fixed rate, 30-year mortgage, $30,000 extra principal, $2,800 monthly rent, $2,800 annual property tax, $3,800 annual management fee, $69 monthly HOA, and 22% marginal federal tax rate.
